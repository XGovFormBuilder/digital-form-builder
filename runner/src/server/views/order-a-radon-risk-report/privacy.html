UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA) Radon Services Privacy Notice

About UKHSA

UKHSA is an executive agency of the government, sponsored by the Department of Health and Social Care (DHSC). The DHSC is the data controller for the personal information we collect, store and use to fulfil our remit.

We are responsible for planning, preventing and responding to external health threats, and providing intellectual, scientific and operational leadership at national and local level, as well as internationally. UKHSA will ensure the nation can respond quickly and at greater scale to deal with pandemics and future threats.

We collect and use personal information to fulfil our remit from the government. UKHSA's responsibilities include providing a range of radon services, which includes ordering radon risk reports and radon measurement packs. These services can be accessed via the Radon Services website (opens in new tab). This privacy notice explains what personal information we collect, use and may share for the purposes of providing you with our radon services.

The information we collect

We collect the following personal information to help us provide the requested service:

  • full name
  • address
  • postcode
  • phone number
  • email address
  • IP address

How we collect your information

This information is provided directly when you place an order for our services or submit an enquiry form via the website.

The purposes we use your information for

This information is used to enable us to provide you with radon services and to support us in our public health duty to protect the population from radon exposure.

How we protect your information

The personal information used by Radon Services is protected in a number of ways.

It is stored on computer systems that have been tested to make sure they are secure and which are kept up-to-date to protect them from viruses and hacking.

We won't share your personal information with any other organisations for marketing, market research or commercial purposes, and we don't pass on your details to other websites.

Your information used by us can only be seen by staff who have been specifically trained to protect your privacy. Strong controls are in place to make sure all these staff can only see the minimum amount of personal information they need to do their job.

Whenever possible, we only use your information in a form that does not directly identify you.

No information that could identify individual people is ever published by UKHSA.

Where we store your information

All personal information used by Radon Services is held in the UK only.

Who we share your information with

We do not routinely share your personal information with external organisations. In limited circumstances, we may share it with other parts of UKHSA if this required to provide our services or improve the service, and only where the law allows us and we only share the minimum necessary amount of information.

Your rights over your information

Under data protection law, you have a number of rights over your personal information. You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of any information we hold about you
  • ask for any information we hold about you to be changed if it is inaccurate
  • ask us to restrict our use of your information, for example, if you think the information we are using is inaccurate
  • object to us using your information (although this is not an absolute right and we may need to continue using it – if so, we will explain why)
  • ask us to delete your information (although this is not an absolute right and we may need to continue to use your information – we will tell you why if this is the case)
  • ask us not to make automated decisions about you without the involvement of one of our staff

You can exercise any of these rights by contacting UKHSA at:

Information Rights Team

You may be asked to provide proof of identity to protect your personal information.

Our legal basis for using your information

UKHSA is allowed to collect and use personal data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. The legal basis we rely on is:

  • GDPR Article 6(1)(e) – processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest

How to find out more or raise a concern

If you have any questions about this privacy notice or how we use your personal information, you can contact us at radon@ukhsa.gov.uk.

If you have any concerns about how personal information is used and protected by UKHSA, you can contact the Department of Health and Social Care's Data Protection Officer at data_protection@dhsc.gov.uk or by writing to:
